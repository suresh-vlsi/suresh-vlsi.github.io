About Me

I am Kusumuru Suresh Kumar, an M.Tech student in Systems & Control Engineering at IIT Bombay, with a background in Electrical and Electronics Engineering.

My technical interests are centered around digital hardware design and verification, with a particular focus on Formal Verification, SystemVerilog Assertions, RTL Design and VLSI CAD.

I enjoy understanding hardware at both the implementation and verification levels—from designing RTL and writing assertions to exploring formal methods, BDDs and symbolic model checking.
